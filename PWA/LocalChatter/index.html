Messages appear here while you're connected. Nothing is saved: when you leave, they're gone.

Effects

Everyone in the chat sees it. You can also type /fire, /earthquake, /lightning or /zalgo 30 (seconds; /zalgo on until /zalgo off).

Favourites

    Debug mode

    Whatever's in the code or tag is sent to the chat.

    Settings

    Open the LocalChatter server's page on this device (for example https://192.168.1.50:8443) and tap Set up LocalChatter, or type its address here.

    Can't connect? Open the server page and accept its certificate, then try again.

    Languages

    Messages in languages you don't understand look like gibberish. Join and leave lines are in System. You can also use /languages and /speak.

    Role

    Your role: member. Type /role to see the others.
