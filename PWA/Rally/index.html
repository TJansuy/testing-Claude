Rally

Tennis for two, straight from one device to the other: no server and no internet needed, just the same Wi-Fi (or one phone's hotspot).

1. Show this to the other player

They scan it with their phone's camera, or with Join a game.

2. Then scan their reply

Scan the host's invite

Show this to the host

They tap Scan their reply. The game starts as soon as you're connected.

How to play
  • Your racket follows your mouse or finger (or the arrow keys). Swing it through the ball: the faster you swing, the harder you hit.
  • Swing upwards to brush the ball with topspin, so it dips into the court. Hitting it while moving down gives backspin, so it floats.
  • Low down the racket face opens up to lift the ball over the net; up high it closes to hit down. Tilt it more with scroll, Q/E or ⤴ ⤵.
  • The ball has to clear the net and land in. One bounce on your side, then you have to hit it back. First to 7, winning by 2.
  • To play someone: one of you taps Host a game, the other scans the QR code that appears, then the host scans the one that appears on the other screen. No camera? Copy and paste the codes instead, e.g. in a message.
